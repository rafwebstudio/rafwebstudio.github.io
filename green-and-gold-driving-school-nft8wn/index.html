About us

Driving lessons across the Hills & Blacktown

Green & Gold Driving School has been teaching learners in Sydney since 2004. We train beginners, test preparation, pre-test assessments and single-item training, depending on your skill level.

We cover Castle Hill, Kellyville, Baulkham Hills, Seven Hills, Blacktown, Rouse Hill, Stanhope Gardens and surrounding areas.
