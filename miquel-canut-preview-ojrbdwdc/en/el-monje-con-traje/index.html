The book

El monje con traje

Finding your purpose in life and true happiness.

Many people live with a gap between the life they want and the life they actually lead. This practical guide, written by Raf Adams from his own experience, helps close the gap between your purpose (the inner «monk») and the world of achievement (the «suit» you wear), so you can live with more inner peace.

  • Amat Editorial2019

Bringing El Monje con Traje into daily life

As well as translating the book, Miquel is a facilitator of El Monje con Traje (The Suited Monk), by Raf Adams. He helps individuals and groups put its ideas into practice: bringing the spirit together with the many demands of everyday life.
