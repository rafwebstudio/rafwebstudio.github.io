Plant medicine processes

A caring space to look within

Personalised therapeutic processes, with preparation, support and integration.

The approach

The therapeutic purpose, always at the centre

The plant medicine processes Miquel guides are personalised therapeutic processes within a group setting.

Each participant prepares individually, according to their intention, with guidelines on physical preparation and lifestyle and a prior health assessment. Support continues for up to a month afterwards, so the process can be integrated and bring real clarity.

How each process is cared for

Four pillars

Preparation

Individual work beforehand on each person's intention.

Care

Medical and physical preparation guidelines, such as diet and lifestyle.

Safe space

Protected spaces run with professionalism, so that nobody feels alone.

Integration

Support for up to a month afterwards, so the process is complete.

The atmosphere

Sobriety and sacredness

The gatherings include moments of reflection through the music, the team's attitude and the setting, with balance and never losing sight of their therapeutic purpose.

These processes do not replace any medical or psychological treatment. Taking part requires a prior interview and assessment.
