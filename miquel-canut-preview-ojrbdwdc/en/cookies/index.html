Cookie policy

Draft written for the preview. It must be completed with the owner's details and reviewed by a professional before the site goes live.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser so that it works properly or to collect information about how it is used.

2. Cookies used on this site

This website does not use analytics, advertising or social media cookies. Fonts and images are served from the site itself. Only technical cookies that are essential for it to work might be used, and these do not require consent.

3. Changes

If analytics tools, embedded videos or other non-technical cookies are added in future, this policy will be updated and a notice will ask for your consent before they are set.

4. How to manage cookies

You can allow, block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5. Contact

For any questions, write to mcanut@miquelcanut.com.
