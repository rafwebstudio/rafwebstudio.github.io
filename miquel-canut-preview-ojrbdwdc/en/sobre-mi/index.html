About me

A practical and spiritual approach

Therapist and life coach with more than 18 years of experience guiding processes of personal growth and inner transformation.

Miquel Canut

Hello, I'm Miquel

For more than 18 years I have trained and walked alongside people who wanted to look at their lives honestly and feel at home within themselves again.

I trained in the United States as a Spiritual Coach and Life Coach, in NLP and in A Course in Miracles. I guide processes of personal growth, such as childhood wounds or trauma, and of inner transformation, integrating different disciplines to support a deep understanding of oneself.

I have also trained as an integration therapist through ICEERS (International Center for Ethnobotanical Education, Research and Service) and directly in the Amazon rainforest, with different teachers. [Pending: Miquel's final wording about this training]

I speak at international events, I am co-creator of the self-help programme Easy Life Easy Money and Spanish translator of The Suited Monk (El Monje con Traje, Editorial Amat). I am also a facilitator of El Monje con Traje (The Suited Monk), by Raf Adams.
