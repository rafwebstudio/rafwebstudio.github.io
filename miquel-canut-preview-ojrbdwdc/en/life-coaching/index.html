The guidance

A combination of practical and spiritual approaches

Miquel's way of guiding people as a therapist and coach brings together the practical and the spiritual.

In his sessions you can explore any area of your life: a disappointment at work, a challenge in a relationship, a money issue, a project you want to develop, a life change, a loss.

Miquel takes you step by step to observe and untangle any inner issue or challenge you need to see clearly, from a perspective rooted in personal experience: accessible, loving and always free of judgement.

For which moments

What you can work on

Work and purpose

A disappointment at work, a project you want to develop or the feeling of having lost your way.

Relationships

A challenge with your partner, your family or someone important to you.

Changes and grief

A life change, a loss, an ending that needs its time.

Money

Money issues that keep repeating and ask to be looked at from within.

Childhood wounds

Personal growth processes that go back a long way, supported with care.

Self-esteem

Recognising your worth again and what you deserve.

What you take away

Transformative sessions from the very first moment

The coaching tools Miquel uses lead you, simply and powerfully, back to your heart, to find direction in your life again.

His guidance has real value: he doesn't want to give you answers, but to encourage you, with love and firmness, to become the master of your own transformation.

  • Clarity about what you are going through
  • A renewed direction for your life
  • Resources that stay with you and that you can apply on your own
  • A safe, warm space, free of judgement
