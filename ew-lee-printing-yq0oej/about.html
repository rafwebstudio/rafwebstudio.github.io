Our story

Over 55 years of helping businesses grow.

For over 55 years, E.W. Lee has been servicing businesses throughout Australia and helping them grow. It started with one person’s artistry.

Our founder Edward Lee brought together his artistic and literary skills to fill a gap in the market. The late 1960s was a period of great change: new businesses and cultural experiences were emerging, and people needed a way to express themselves and their services to their communities.

Back then, bringing visuals and text to life required real artistry. There were no stock photos, limited fonts, and mainstream desktop publishing was still decades away. Edward’s unique skill set of drawing, painting, lettering and layout design made him highly sought after, and soon the city was awash with his work on signage, books, magazines and other publications.

Today, Katrina and the next generation of print crafters are proud to still be a voice for our community: for the businesses, the not-for-profits, artists, authors and designers, the change makers and the storytellers.
