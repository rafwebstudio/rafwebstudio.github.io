Our story

A family print business with an artist’s eye.

Our founder Edward Lee brought together his artistic and literary skills to fill a gap in the market in the late 1960s. His drawing, painting, lettering and layout design were soon in demand, and his work appeared on signage, books, magazines and other publications.

Today, Katrina and the next generation of print crafters carry that on, combining a craft mentality with the latest technologies and thoughtful design.
