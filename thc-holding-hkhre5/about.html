Who we are

A family business in metal, for more than 35 years.

THC Holding Pty Ltd is an Australian company that exports scrap metal for recycling, obtaining metals and steel from decommissioned and demolished industrial installations and shipping bulk quantities to overseas steel mills.

Our history

From T&T Metal Trading to THC Holding.

A long history in metal recycling, supporting local businesses along the way.

THC HoldingScrap steel ready for export
More than 35 years ago

T&T Metal Trading

The business began as T&T Metal Trading, a family business in metal recycling.

2011

Incorporated as THC Holding

The business was incorporated as THC Holding Pty Ltd, focused on exporting scrap metal for recycling.

Today

Monthly bulk shipments to Asia

Processing yards in Port Kembla, NSW and Fremantle, WA, shipping to 10 markets across Asia, with head office in Pyrmont, Sydney.

How we work

Sustainability and responsibility.

THC Holding works with the demolition industry to help build new communities around the world.

Reuse, not waste

We make sure this precious raw material is used and reused, which reduces our environmental impact.

Market reach

Regular contact with end-user mills across many markets helps us stay ahead of market changes.

Independent

As a private company we aren’t tied to long-term fixed supply contracts, so we can chase the best spot price.
