What we do

Scrap metal recycling and bulk shipment experts.

We buy and market ferrous and non-ferrous metals from Australian demolition, decommissioning and industrial projects, and ship them in bulk to steel mills across Asia.

01

Demolition & decommissioning scrap

THC Holding works together with the demolition industry, obtaining metals and steel from decommissioned and demolished industrial installations, from power stations and oil refineries to processing plants.

  • For demolition firms selling scrap from industrial and property rehabilitation projects
  • Experience with power stations, refineries, plants and port infrastructure
  • Large and oversize items, including whole-unit coal loaders
02

Ferrous & non-ferrous metal marketing

We work with Australian companies to recycle ferrous and non-ferrous metals for export worldwide, marketing metals as a one stop shop, direct from supplier to end-user mill.

  • Regular contact with end-user mills in many different markets
  • Not tied to long-term fixed supply contracts
  • Focused on the best price on the South East Asian spot market
03

Bulk export shipping

We export monthly bulk shipments from our processing yards in Port Kembla, NSW and Fremantle, WA. Scrap is shipped to steel mills in Asia to be refined, melted and resold.

  • Port Kembla Yard: Lot 1, Old Port Road, Port Kembla NSW
  • Fremantle Yard: 75 Edison Circuit, Forrestdale WA
  • Markets including China, Vietnam, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Bangladesh, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Taiwan
04

Redundant steel materials

Large-scale redundant steel finds a second life as raw material for new steel products.

  • Oil & gas pipe
  • H beams and railway lines
  • Mill rollers
  • Mining equipment surplus
