The project

El Último Viaje del Ego is releasing the identity of fear. That identity that will not let you be yourself.

El último viaje del ego will help you clear the mind of limiting self-judgements that keep you from connecting with deservingness and valuing yourself.

A practical process to let go of the fear of releasing your identity, that identity built from beliefs in order to get the love you do not give yourself. That life from scarcity. If you beg for love you can never be abundant.

Your only purpose in life is to love yourself and be happy. Not to please others, not to live for others, and not to live from guilt.

The ego is a cluster of fears and unfelt circumstances that keep you from connecting and living from who you are. You live in the mismatch between who you are and who you have always pretended to be.

Removing the obstacles that keep you from experiencing who you really are, so you can be at peace and know yourself. Those fears are blocks your own ego creates, and they stop your life from flowing with clarity, purpose, serenity and ease.

El Último Viaje del Ego is a practical process to recognise and identify the identity patterns that shape how we think, feel and act. A path to understand how they are built, which needs and fears sustain them, and how they limit the way we live and relate.

Through exploring identity, fear, love, need, dependence and essence, we open the possibility of questioning what we have learned, releasing automatic patterns and reclaiming the freedom to choose how we want to live.

We do this through retreats, workshops, therapy and individual guidance, creating spaces to experience, understand and integrate new ways of living from your being.

It is not only about understanding what is happening to you, but about recognising where you are living from and opening the possibility of doing it another way so you can change it.
