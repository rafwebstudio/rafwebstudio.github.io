About Melania

From traditional Yoga to everyday life

Yoga, Meditation and Ayurveda teacher trainer with more than 10 years of experience guiding processes of self-knowledge and emotional healing.

She trained in traditional schools in India, exploring different paths of knowledge and practice. She has studied Advaita Vedanta in depth with Maa Gyaan, a teacher and spiritual guide with more than 30 years of experience, and Ayurveda with Dr Neetu Singh, an Ayurvedic doctor with more than 20 years of experience.

She has helped dozens of people regain confidence, health, vitality, clarity, calm, motivation and serenity, in both individual and group processes. She leads retreats and immersions in India and Europe, integrating meditation, breathwork, movement and self-observation.

She is the author of the Italian book Chakra. Dalla saggezza yogica alla pratica quotidiana, a work that brings the wisdom of Yoga into everyday life.

What it can bring you

Resources you take home

«My aim is not for you to feel better only during a session: it is for you to take away something you can keep using when you get home.»

It is not about doing more, it is about understanding what you need, discovering what helps you and learning to use it yourself.

In her theory and practice workshops and in individual sessions, Melania brings together food, body, habits and breathing. You will learn to listen to your body's signals, to understand how what you eat affects your energy, digestion, rest and mental clarity, and to use different ways of breathing to calm down, energise, recover energy, focus or release tension.

No rigid diets or fixed rules: practical criteria and simple practices so you can learn to make better choices for yourself in your daily life.

Melania teaches practical resources to:

  • Manage anxiety and stress better, learning resources to regain calm and lower your level of tension.
  • Understand and manage your emotions, so that anger, frustration, sadness or guilt, among others, don't carry you away and you can respond differently.
  • Regain energy and vitality, learning to recognise what is wearing you down and what you need to feel stronger and more motivated again.
  • Sleep better and relax more, bringing in habits and practices that support deeper, more restorative rest.
  • Improve digestive health, learning to identify which habits, rhythms and ways of eating support better digestion and a greater sense of lightness.
  • Support hormonal changes, with tools for eating, rest, movement and self-care adapted to stages such as the menstrual cycle, perimenopause and menopause.
  • Improve concentration and the ability to focus, reducing distraction and learning to direct your attention better.
  • Reconnect with your body, learning to listen to its signals, recognise your needs and notice tiredness, tension or over-activation sooner.
  • Improve physical performance, learning to make better use of your breath, attention, energy and body awareness.
  • Improve your relationship with your sexuality and your body, regaining presence, body connection, self-knowledge and confidence.
  • Go through times of change with more clarity, reconnecting with your needs, your values and what you truly want.
  • Regain motivation and meaning, when you feel you have lost direction or are running on autopilot.
  • Make self-care real and sustainable, learning small practices you can bring into your daily life without adding more pressure to it.
1:1 Mentoring

Individual guidance

We start from what is happening to you, what you want to change and what you need right now.

We can work on things such as:

  • anxiety and stress
  • tiredness and lack of energy
  • difficulty resting
  • digestion and food
  • difficult emotions
  • lack of concentration
  • feeling disconnected from your body
  • habits you want to change
  • moments of change or feeling stuck
  • loss of motivation or direction

During the session we will combine different resources according to your needs: self-knowledge, breathing, attention, movement, habits, food and body practices.

You will take away: a clearer understanding of what you need and a set of practices and concrete guidelines to start applying it in your life.

Includes follow-up afterwards to support the integration of what you have worked on.
