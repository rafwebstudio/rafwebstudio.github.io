About Melania

From traditional Yoga to everyday life

Yoga, Meditation and Ayurveda teacher trainer with more than 10 years of experience guiding processes of self-knowledge and emotional healing.

She trained in traditional schools in India, exploring different paths of knowledge and practice. She has studied Advaita Vedanta in depth with Maa Gyaan, a teacher and spiritual guide with more than 30 years of experience, and Ayurveda with Dr Neetu Singh, an Ayurvedic doctor with more than 20 years of experience.

She has helped dozens of people regain confidence, health, vitality, clarity, calm, motivation and serenity, in both individual and group processes. She leads retreats and immersions in India and Europe, integrating meditation, breathwork, movement and self-observation.

She is the author of the Italian book Chakra. Dalla saggezza yogica alla pratica quotidiana, a work that brings the wisdom of Yoga into everyday life.
