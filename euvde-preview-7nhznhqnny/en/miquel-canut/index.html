About Miquel

A practical and spiritual approach

Therapist and Life Coach with more than 18 years of experience. Trained in the USA as a Spiritual Coach and Life Coach, in NLP and in A Course in Miracles.

He guides processes of personal growth (childhood wounds, trauma) and inner transformation, integrating different disciplines to support a deep understanding of oneself.

He has trained in therapeutic support with plant medicines and as an integration therapist through ICEERS (International Center for Ethnobotanical Education, Research and Service) and directly in the Amazon rainforest. He speaks at international events.

He is also the Spanish translator of the book The Suited Monk (El Monje con Traje, Editorial Amat) and a facilitator of the El Monje con Traje method created by Raf Adams.

miquelcanut.com @miquel_canut
Life coaching and therapeutic guidance

Becoming the master of your own transformation

Miquel's way of guiding people as a therapist and coach combines a practical and a spiritual approach. In his sessions you can explore any area of your life: a disappointment at work, a challenge in a relationship, a money issue, a project you want to develop, a life change, a loss…

Miquel takes you step by step to observe and untangle any inner issue or challenge you need to see clearly, from a perspective rooted in personal experience: accessible, loving and always free of judgement.

The coaching tools he uses lead you, simply and powerfully, back to your heart and to finding direction in your life again, with resources that stay with you and that you can keep applying on your own. His guidance does not seek to give you answers, but to invite you, with love and firmness, to become the master of your own transformation.
