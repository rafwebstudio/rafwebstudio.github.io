Welcome

A unique journey to the heart of India

Set off on a unique journey to the heart of India over two fascinating weeks, full of culture, spirituality, personal growth workshops, yoga and meditation.

El Último Viaje del Ego is an awakening to our identity, fears and beliefs that will take you to India to look within, question what we have identified with and open up to a deeper recognition of who we are.

The retreat will combine different tools for exploration and transformation: meditation, breathwork, movement, self-observation, group work, workshops and self-knowledge work, guided by Melania Di Vara and Miquel Canut.

You don't need any previous experience of yoga, meditation or spiritual paths. The retreat is open to anyone who simply feels the wish to stop and go deeper into themselves.

Rishikesh, by the Ganges
01The outer journey

India, its landscapes and its tradition

India, its landscapes, its culture and its spiritual tradition. We won't simply visit places; we will live India as a space for exploration and transformation.

02The inner journey

Observe, question and let go

A chance to observe, question and let go of some of the identities, stories and conditioning we live from. From there you can connect with your Self, with worthiness and self-value.

Our base

Rishikesh

During the retreat we will stay in a hotel with every comfort, which will be our home and practice space. Rishikesh lets us alternate inner work with being by the Ganges and soaking up the atmosphere of one of India's great spiritual cities.

  • Yoga, breathwork and meditation practices.
  • El Último Viaje del Ego workshops and sessions.
  • Group activities and experiences.
  • Time for reflection and personal observation.
  • Moments of rest and integration.
Practice space

The yoga room, facing the Himalayas

A bright space with mountain views, where every morning we will practise yoga, breathwork and meditation as part of the inner journey.

Excursions included
Experiences

What the retreat includes

The programme will include different spiritual experiences and excursions over these 14 days.

  • Traditional rituals by the Ganges.
  • Visit to Parmarth Niketan, one of India's most important ashrams.
  • Meeting with Maa Gyaan, a recognised spiritual guide for 30 years.
  • Vashista Cave.
  • Visits to temples facing the Himalayas.
  • Free time to explore the town.
  • Food experiences and outings beyond the hotel.
  • Nature walks around Rishikesh.
Time to stop

Times of silence and rest

We don't want to fill every moment of the trip. There will also be free time, rest and silence, to integrate the experience and enjoy India without rushing: strolling through Rishikesh, enjoying its cafés and restaurants, having Ayurvedic treatments or massages, resting, sitting by the Ganges or exploring the town on your own.

Some of these experiences will be optional and not included in the retreat price.
